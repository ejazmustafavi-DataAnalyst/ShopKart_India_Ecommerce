## 📊 ShopKart India E-Commerce Sales, Product & Customer Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)
 
## 📌 Project Overview
This interactive **Power BI Dashboard** provides an end-to-end business intelligence solution analyzing **Sales Performance**, **Product Profitability & Returns**, and **Customer Demographics & Behavior**. Designed with a clean, executive-ready UI and dynamic cross-filtering, this 3-page report empowers stakeholders to track KPIs, identify high-performing products, mitigate return rates, and optimize regional sales strategies.

---

## 🎯 Key Business Objectives
* **Sales Tracking:** Monitor overall revenue, cost, net profit, profit margins, and monthly/regional sales trends.
* **Product Optimization:** Identify top revenue-generating products alongside high-return items to improve inventory quality and margin efficiency.
* **Customer Segmentation:** Analyze purchasing behavior across age groups, cities, and top-spending/most-visited customers.
* **Interactive Exploration:** Enable granular slicing by **State**, **Year/Month**, **Product Category**, and **Payment Method**.

## 📈 Dashboard Pages & Key Insights

### 1. Sales Analysis Dashboard:
Provides a high-level financial summary and temporal/geographical revenue breakdown.
* **Headline KPIs:**
  * **Total Revenue:** ₹2,290,997 (~2.29M)
  * **Total Cost:** ₹1,721,548 (~1.72M)
  * **Total Profit:** ₹569,449 (~569.45K)
  * **Profit Margin %:** 24.86%
  * **Total Orders:** 279
* **Key Visuals & Insights:**
  * **Monthly Revenue Trend:** **January (₹0.54M)** and **March (₹0.47M)** record the highest sales peaks, followed by a steady mid-year performance and a dip in Q4.
  * **Regional Performance:** **East (₹537.09K)** and **North (₹518.16K)** lead regional revenue generation, while **North-East (₹169.74K)** shows the lowest volume.
  * **Category Contribution:** **Electronics** dominates the revenue share at **73.33% (₹1,680.10K)**, followed by **Home & Kitchen (13.57%)** and **Fashion (9.32%)**.

  * **Margin %:** 49.98%
  * **Total Orders:** 279
  * **Total Returns:** 45 (**Return Rate:** 16.13%)
  * **Total Products:** 15
* **Key Visuals & Insights:**
  * **Top 5 Selling Products:** **Smartphone X1 (₹699.60K)** and **Laptop Air 14 (₹588.36K)** are the primary revenue drivers.

### 3. Customers Analysis:
Explores customer loyalty, high-value buyers, age demographics, and geographic distribution across India.
* **Key Visuals & Insights:**
  * **Top 5 Visited Customers:** Customer **C016** leads in order frequency with **22 orders**, followed by **C013 (17)** and **C018 (16)**.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling, report design, and interactive visualizations.
* **Power Query (M):** Data cleaning, type transformations, and custom column calculations.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Custom measures for `Total Revenue`, `Total Cost`, `Total Profit`, `Profit Margin %`, `Avg Margin`, `Total Returns`, and `Return %`.
